1. Please introduce yourself.
Heya, I'm Luna. I'm an autistic trans girl and furry from germany who turned into a therian kitty mess throughout the years. I love programming, messing around with silly stuff and just dipping my paws into whatever I find interesting on any given day!
2. How long have you been making websites?
I made my first HTML sites at 7 years old, but learned it properly in IT class in grade 8, along with PHP to boot. That eventually turned into me begrudgingly becoming a React dev professionally for a few years, a career path I will never touch again.
3. And what got you into the hobby?
As a kid, the thought of essentially writing a letter with what's on my mind and having others read it from all over the world felt like impossible magic to me. I didn't even know what CSS was at the time, and poor little luna thought having an index.html on her desktop meant that thousands of other people were looking at her site every day x3
4. What kind of website are you most interested in?
Definetely indie sites. I love seeing other people's cool designs, creativity, their OCs, shrines and interests. It's a bit of passion and personality in this hypermodernized and -commercialized shithole we call the internet today.
5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?
With this one specifically, I kinda just made a plain HTML site and played around with some CSS after, until I got something minimalistic that looked nice. Usually I like to do a bit of planing though, at least when it comes to layouts and pages, so that I don't end up refactoring my CSS everytime I wanna add something else.
6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.
Oof... I don't know if I have any specific ones for this site. I am very influenced by the vibe of MySpace (or nowadays SpaceHey) pages, and whatever I pick up when hitting 'random' on webrings. But there itn't any particular inspirtion for this page, I just messed around with ideas and landed on this design.
